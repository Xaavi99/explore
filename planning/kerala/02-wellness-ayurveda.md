# Model 2 — Wellness & Ayurveda Retreat

**9 nights.** Built around one long, uninterrupted stay at a genuine
Ayurveda centre — not a spa menu bolted onto a sightseeing trip. Transfers
are kept as short and as few as possible on purpose: the point of this
itinerary is rest, and every extra hour in a car works against that.

**Ideal traveller**: guests prioritising a real therapeutic programme over
ground covered — often returning visitors to India, or first-timers
specifically booking for wellness rather than sightseeing.

## Route & nights

| Stop | Nights | Why this many |
|---|---|---|
| Trivandrum arrival / short transfer | 1 | Soft-landing night close to the airport before the retreat begins |
| Ayurveda retreat, Kollam district | 6 | The minimum for a genuine short programme with visible results — physician consultation, 5 full treatment days, and a rest day built in |
| Varkala | 2 | Coastal wind-down, gentle reintroduction to normal life before flying home |

**Gateway: Trivandrum (TRV)**, not Kochi — this is a south-Kerala itinerary
end to end, and routing through Kochi would mean an unnecessary extra 2–3
hours of driving each way for no benefit. See the [gateway logic](README.md#arrival-logistics-the-gateway-decision).

## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive Trivandrum, transfer to retreat** (≈50 km / ~1.5 hrs to the
Kollam district). Check-in, orientation, light early dinner per the
programme's diet. No physician consultation on arrival day — guests should
land settled, not rushed into a clinical assessment jet-lagged.

**Day 2 — Physician consultation.** A qualified Ayurvedic physician assesses
constitution (*prakriti*) and current imbalance (*vikriti*) and prescribes
the programme: which treatments, in what sequence, and the accompanying
diet. This is not optional and not skippable — it's what separates a
genuine centre from a resort spa, and it's why the property matters more
than the itinerary here.

**Days 3–7 — Treatment programme.** Five full days on the prescribed
regimen: daily *abhyanga* (oil massage) and/or *shirodhara* (oil-pouring)
sessions depending on the physician's plan, morning yoga, prescribed meals
timed around treatment, and one rest day with no treatment scheduled (the
physician will place this — usually mid-programme, not at the end). No
excursions during this block; that's the model, not an oversight.

**Day 8 — Drive to Varkala** (≈35 km / ~1 hr). A short, easy transfer.
Afternoon free to walk the cliff path.

**Day 9 — Varkala, unstructured.** Cliff-top walk, the mineral springs at
Papanasam Beach, or simply the beach. The point of this day is that nothing
is booked.

**Day 10 — Depart.** Transfer to Trivandrum (≈45 min).

## Stays

**Retreat (Kollam district)**
- *Kalari Kovilakom* (CGH Earth) — a restored 18th-century palace run
  purely as an Ayurveda retreat, physician-led, no sightseeing distraction
  by design. The reference pick for this model.
- *Kalari Rasayana* (CGH Earth) — sister property, similar programme
  structure, smaller and more intimate; the alternate if Kovilakom is full
  or a guest wants a quieter setting.

**Varkala**
- *Vivenda dos Palhaços* — small, design-led, cliff-adjacent boutique
  property; a good match for guests coming out of a quiet retreat who don't
  want to jump straight into a large resort.
- A CGH Earth or Taj-affiliated Varkala property as the larger-scale
  alternate.

## Food & culture

Meals at the retreat are prescribed, not chosen — part of the treatment,
built around the physician's assessment (typically vegetarian, spiced for
digestion, timed with treatment rather than around a normal meal clock).
Varkala's two free days are the place to reintroduce normal Kerala food:
the cliff-top cafés, and if the timing allows, a toddy-shop lunch on the
way out.

## Geography & highlights

The Kollam backwater district is quieter and greener than the tourist
backwater circuit further north — part of why it suits a retreat rather
than a touring stop. Varkala's laterite cliff, the only formation of its
kind on the Kerala coast, gives the wind-down days a dramatic setting
without requiring any activity to enjoy it.

## Pacing note

This model breaks two of the general rules on purpose, deliberately: the
"unstructured day per 5–6 days" rule is folded into the retreat's own rest
day (placed by the physician, not by us), and there is no hill stop at all
— altitude and a wellness programme don't mix well, and the entire point is
minimising transfers. Every transfer in this itinerary is under 1.5 hours.
