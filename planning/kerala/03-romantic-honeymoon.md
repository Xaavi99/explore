# Model 3 — Romantic & Honeymoon Kerala

**8 nights.** Fewer stops than the Signature Classic, longer at each one,
and every choice — the houseboat, the last beach stop — picked for privacy
rather than range. Open-jaw: arrive Kochi, depart Trivandrum, so the route
never doubles back south to north again.

**Ideal traveller**: couples and honeymooners who want scenery and intimacy
over ground covered — fewer check-ins, more time to actually be somewhere.

## Route & nights

| Stop | Nights | Why this many |
|---|---|---|
| Kochi | 1 | Soft-landing night only — this model saves its time for the stops that matter more to a couple |
| Munnar | 2 | Hill-country romance: sunrise, tea gardens, a private candlelit dinner among the estates |
| Thekkady | 1 | A short, gentle interim stop that also breaks up what would otherwise be two long drives back to back |
| Kumarakom → Alleppey (private houseboat) | 1 | A private *kettuvallam* for two — the single most romantic night in any Kerala itinerary |
| Poovar Island | 3 | The private, secluded close: floating cottages, a sandbar between backwater and sea, no resort strip |

**Open-jaw routing**: arrive **Kochi (COK)**, depart **Trivandrum (TRV)** —
avoids retracing the same 300+ km of road at the end of the trip. See the
[gateway logic](README.md#arrival-logistics-the-gateway-decision).

## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive Kochi.** Transfer, check in, rest. No activity.

**Day 2 — Drive to Munnar** (≈130 km / ~4 hrs). Arrive by mid-afternoon,
private welcome tea at the estate.

**Day 3 — Munnar.** Sunrise trek for two with a naturalist guide, private
tea-estate walk and tasting, evening candlelit dinner arranged among the
tea gardens or on a private terrace.

**Day 4 — Drive to Thekkady** (≈110 km / ~3.5–4 hrs). Afternoon spice
plantation walk together; short evening rather than a full performance
booking — this stop is deliberately light.

**Day 5 — Drive to Kumarakom, board houseboat** (≈120 km / ~3 hrs). Board
by late afternoon; private *kettuvallam*, just the couple and crew, sunset
cruise into the Vembanad backwaters, dinner cooked onboard.

**Day 6 — Disembark Alleppey, drive to Poovar** (≈150–160 km / ~4 hrs, the
longest single leg in this model — positioned here, mid-trip, rather than
on arrival or departure day). Arrive Poovar by late afternoon; boat transfer
across the estuary to the floating cottage.

**Day 7 — Poovar, unstructured.** Nothing booked. Optional: couple's spa
treatment, sunset boat ride on the estuary.

**Day 8 — Poovar, free / optional activities.** Paragliding at Varkala can
be arranged as a day excursion (≈1 hr each way) for guests who want one
active day; otherwise, another unstructured day.

**Day 9 — Depart Trivandrum** (≈1 hr from Poovar).

## Stays

**Kochi**
- *Brunton Boatyard* (CGH Earth) — one night only, but worth the setting for
  an arrival night.

**Munnar**
- *Windermere Estate* — private plantation-bungalow feel, the right scale
  for a couple rather than a large resort.

**Thekkady**
- *Spice Village* (CGH Earth) — cottage rooms, kept low-key by design for
  this shorter stop.

**Kumarakom / houseboat**
- Private premium *kettuvallam*, two-cabin minimum booked as a
  single-couple charter (not a shared or multi-cabin boat) — this is the
  detail that makes the night actually private.

**Poovar Island**
- A floating-cottage or private-pool-villa property on the Poovar
  estuary — the defining choice of this itinerary. Access is by boat only,
  which is itself part of the appeal.

## Food & culture

Candlelit dinners arranged at the Munnar estate and again at Poovar;
onboard houseboat cooking (fresh fish, Kerala backwater-style curries)
served for two rather than as part of a larger group sitting; a spice
tasting at Thekkady the couple does together rather than as part of a
guided group tour.

## Geography & highlights

Munnar's tea-garden sunrise; Periyar's forest edge at Thekkady, seen
briefly rather than as a full reserve day; Vembanad Lake at its stillest at
dusk from the houseboat deck; Poovar's sandbar, where the Neyyar River,
backwaters and Arabian Sea meet — one of the most photogenic, and most
private, settings on the Kerala coast.

## Pacing note

The one long drive (Alleppey→Poovar, ~4 hrs) sits in the middle of the
trip, not at the start or the end — guests are neither jet-lagged nor tired
from a long-haul flight home when they take it. Everything before it is
short; everything after it (3 nights, one stop) requires no further
movement at all.
