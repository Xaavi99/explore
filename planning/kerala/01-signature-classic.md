# Model 1 — Signature Classic Kerala

**8 nights.** The full range of Kerala in one comfortably paced loop: hill
tea country, wildlife reserve, one backwater night, and a beach close before
departure. Built for a first visit — nothing is skipped, but nothing is
rushed either.

**Ideal traveller**: first-time visitors to South India, couples or friends
travelling together, anyone who wants "the real Kerala" without a punishing
schedule.

## Route & nights

| Stop | Nights | Why this many |
|---|---|---|
| Kochi | 2 | Soft-landing day after arrival, then a full day for Fort Kochi/heritage — no need to rush this on the way through |
| Munnar | 2 | Tea country needs a sunrise and a full day of estate/park time; one night here is never enough |
| Thekkady | 1 | Periyar is a single strong day (boat safari or forest walk) — one night is right, two starts to feel repetitive |
| Kumarakom → Alleppey (houseboat) | 1 | One overnight on the water, boarding at Kumarakom, cruising through to Alleppey by morning |
| Marari Beach | 2 | The wind-down stop, close to the airport, with a genuinely unstructured day built in |

Arrival and departure both through **Kochi (COK)** — see the [gateway logic
in the overview](README.md#arrival-logistics-the-gateway-decision). This is
the model's natural fit: everything routes in a loop out of and back to
Kochi with no backtracking.

## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive Kochi.** Land, transfer to hotel (20–40 min from the
airport depending on property), rest. No activity scheduled — soft-landing
day per the pacing rules.

**Day 2 — Fort Kochi.** Full day on foot through the heritage quarter:
Chinese fishing nets, Mattancherry Palace, Paradesi Synagogue and Jew Town,
St. Francis Church. Evening Kathakali performance, arriving early to watch
the makeup applied.

**Day 3 — Drive to Munnar** (≈130 km / ~4 hrs, hill road). Depart after
breakfast; the drive itself is scenic (waterfalls, spice-estate roadside
stops) so it isn't dead time. Arrive by mid-afternoon, rest of the day free
at the estate.

**Day 4 — Munnar full day.** Early start for Eravikulam National Park
(Nilgiri tahr, high shola grassland) before the mist burns off, then a
guided tea estate walk and tasting in the afternoon. Optional sunrise trek
the following dawn for energetic guests.

**Day 5 — Drive to Thekkady** (≈110 km / ~3.5–4 hrs via Kumily). Depart
mid-morning after a relaxed breakfast; arrive for lunch. Afternoon spice
plantation walk. Evening Kalaripayattu & Kathakali double performance.

**Day 6 — Periyar, then drive to Kumarakom** (≈120 km / ~3 hrs). Early boat
safari on Periyar Lake — best light and wildlife odds first thing — then
depart for Kumarakom after an early lunch, arriving in time to board the
houseboat before dusk.

**Day 7 — Overnight houseboat, Vembanad Lake.** Cruise from Kumarakom
through the Vembanad backwaters, meals cooked onboard, mooring before dusk
per backwater regulations. Disembark at Alleppey mid-morning; short transfer
(≈35–45 min) to Marari Beach.

**Day 8 — Marari Beach, unstructured.** No activity booked. This is the
model's built-in free day — the beach, the property, nothing else.

**Day 9 — Depart.** Transfer to Kochi airport (≈1.5–2 hrs).

## Stays

**Kochi**
- *Brunton Boatyard* (CGH Earth) — waterfront, colonial-era shipyard
  conversion, harbour views straight onto the fishing nets. The reference
  pick.
- *Taj Malabar* — the dependable alternate, also waterfront, on Willingdon
  Island.

**Munnar**
- *Windermere Estate* — small, independently run plantation-bungalow
  property with genuinely personal service; the most distinctive option in
  town.
- *Fragrant Nature Munnar* or a CGH Earth-affiliated estate stay for a
  larger-property alternative.

**Thekkady**
- *Spice Village* (CGH Earth) — cottage-style rooms set in a working spice
  garden, closest in feel to the plantation walk guests do that afternoon.

**Kumarakom / houseboat**
- Private *kettuvallam* (converted rice barge) with dedicated crew and cook
  — a CGH Earth or equivalent premium operator, booked directly rather than
  through a jetty broker for consistent quality and safety standards.

**Marari Beach**
- *Marari Beach Resort* (CGH Earth) — the original, cottage-style, right on
  the fishing village beach it's named for.
- *Vivenda dos Palhaços*-style independent boutique for a smaller,
  design-led alternative if available in the area.

## Food & culture

Onboard houseboat meals cooked fresh from fish bought that morning; a toddy
shop stop can be worked into the Alleppey/Kumarakom day for tapioca and fish
curry, karimeen and duck roast; the Munnar estate tasting covers grades that
never reach export; Thekkady's spice walk ends in a tasting and direct
purchase from growers. Kathakali/Kalaripayattu evenings are booked with
small venues and an explainer beforehand so the performance actually lands
for a first-time audience.

## Geography & highlights

Tea gardens contoured across the Munnar hills since the 1880s; Eravikulam's
high shola grassland and the Nilgiri tahr found nowhere else; Periyar's
drowned forest still standing in the lake formed by the 1895 dam; Vembanad,
India's longest lake, opening from narrow canal to open water at Kumarakom;
Marari's working fishing village, catamarans still launched from the sand
each morning.

## Pacing note

This is the template the other four models are variations on: a hill stop,
one reserve/nature stop, one water night, one coastal close — always in that
order, never doubling back. The only genuinely long drive (Kochi→Munnar) sits
right at the start when guests are freshest, and every subsequent transfer is
under 4 hours.
