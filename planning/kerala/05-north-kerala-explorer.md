# Model 5 — North Kerala Explorer

**7 nights.** Malabar, not the well-trodden south: Theyyam ritual, India's
best-preserved coastal fort, coffee hills instead of tea, and a wildlife
sanctuary that connects straight into Karnataka's reserves. This model also
uses a genuinely better gateway than Kochi — see below.

**Ideal traveller**: repeat visitors who've done a standard Kerala trip
already, or culture-first travellers for whom Theyyam alone is worth the
detour north.

## A better gateway for this model specifically

Kochi is the wrong airport for this itinerary — it's a 5–6 hour drive from
Wayanad and further from Kannur. North Kerala has its own two airports, and
both are genuinely better fits:

- **Kannur International (CNN)** — opened 2018, with direct Gulf service
  (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Riyadh, Jeddah among others), and roughly 20–30
  minutes from Kannur town, under an hour from Bekal. The right arrival
  point for this itinerary.
- **Calicut International (CCJ)** — the older, more established North
  Kerala gateway, similarly Gulf-connected, and about 1.5–2 hours from
  Wayanad. The right departure point, avoiding a drive back to Kannur.

Routing **arrive Kannur, depart Calicut** (open-jaw) means this entire
itinerary never touches Kochi and never redrives a road — genuinely the
simplest logistics of any of the five models, which is itself worth telling
the client.

## Route & nights

| Stop | Nights | Why this many |
|---|---|---|
| Kannur & Bekal | 4 | Deliberately generous — Theyyam performances run on a shrine's schedule, not a tourist's, and a shorter stay risks missing one entirely |
| Wayanad | 3 | Caves, a peak trek, a dam and a wildlife sanctuary genuinely need three days, not a rushed two |

## Day by day

**Day 1 — Arrive Kannur.** Transfer to Bekal/Kannur base (≈40–60 min). Rest;
no activity.

**Day 2 — Bekal Fort & coast.** Bekal Fort in the morning — India's
largest and best-preserved coastal fort, laterite ramparts direct above the
sea. Afternoon at **Muzhappilangad Drive-In Beach**, one of only a few
beaches in India where driving on the sand is legal — a genuinely unusual
half-day.

**Day 3 — Kannur town & craft.** St. Angelo's Fort, a handloom and weaving
cooperative visit (Kannur's looms supplied cloth well beyond Kerala), and
Payyambalam Beach in the late afternoon.

**Day 4 — Theyyam, timing-dependent.** This day is scheduled around the
Theyyam calendar rather than fixed in advance — performances (mainly
December–April) are tracked and confirmed close to travel dates, at shrine
timings that can mean a pre-dawn or late-night start. Briefing on
photography etiquette beforehand; this is a live ritual, not a staged show.

**Day 5 — Drive to Wayanad** (≈95 km / ~2–2.5 hrs via Mananthavady). A
short, easy transfer. Afternoon at **Pookode Lake** — a gentle walk, good
after a travel morning.

**Day 6 — Wayanad, active day.** **Edakkal Caves** (petroglyphs over six
thousand years old, a 45-minute climb to reach them) in the morning;
**Chembra Peak** trek in the afternoon for guests wanting it (permit
required, moderate fitness, the heart-shaped lake partway up), or the
gentler **Soochipara Waterfalls** walk as the lower-effort alternative.

**Day 7 — Wayanad wildlife & coffee.** Early wildlife sanctuary safari
(Muthanga or Tholpetty range — elephant, gaur, sambar regularly seen), then
a coffee and spice estate walk finishing in a cupping session in the
afternoon.

**Day 8 — Depart Calicut** (≈1.5–2 hrs from Wayanad).

## Stays

**Kannur & Bekal**
- *Neeleshwar Hermitage* — independent, beachfront, thatched-cottage
  boutique property near Bekal; the most distinctive option on this coast
  and the reference pick.
- *Ezhara Beach House* or a comparable smaller heritage stay as the
  alternate.

**Wayanad**
- *Tranquil* (CGH Earth) — a working plantation homestay, coffee and spice
  estate, genuinely lived-in rather than built for tourism.
- *Vythiri Village* or *Vythiri Resort* for a larger-format alternative
  with tree-house or cottage options.

## Food & culture

Theyyam itself is the cultural centrepiece — a possession ritual found
nowhere else on earth, not a performance booked to a schedule. The handloom
cooperative and coffee-estate cupping are both hands-on rather than
observational. North Malabar cuisine differs meaningfully from the south —
Kannur's biryani tradition and Malabar-style seafood are worth calling out
specifically to guests who assume Kerala food is uniform.

## Geography & highlights

Bekal's fort ramparts rising directly from the sea; the six-thousand-year
petroglyphs at Edakkal, some in scripts never fully deciphered; Chembra's
heart-shaped lake; the Wayanad sanctuary's direct ecological connection into
Bandipur and Nagarhole across the Karnataka border, part of one of South
India's most important contiguous elephant habitats.

## Pacing note

Every transfer in this model is under 2.5 hours — the lowest of any of the
five — precisely because using Kannur and Calicut as dedicated gateways
removes the long haul to and from Kochi entirely. The one deliberately
flexible day (Theyyam) is placed mid-stay, with three other days around it
absorbing any schedule shift without disturbing the Wayanad leg.
